- **Send to Slicer**: PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer (renamed from *Send to PrusaSlicer*).
- Finds installed and unzipped/portable slicers automatically (running programs, programs Windows has seen run, "Open with", typical folders); alpha/beta/RC/nightly marked *pre-release*.
- Slicer list with *Automatic (newest stable version)* or a fixed choice; path shown underneath.
- The ribbon icon and About window show the logo of the chosen slicer.
- Arrange: A is sent directly to the slicer's 3D view, after any question the slicer asks.
- About… in the menu; Buy me a coffee link.
